Who Is Anthony Edwards?

Anthony Edwards is an American professional basketball player known for his explosive athleticism, scoring ability and aggressive playing style.

Nicknamed "Ant-Man," Edwards plays shooting guard and has become a leading figure for the Minnesota Timberwolves in the NBA.

His game combines powerful drives to the basket, three-point shooting, transition scoring and highlight-reel dunks.
